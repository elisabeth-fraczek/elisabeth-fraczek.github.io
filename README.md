## About me

I am a physicist with a PhD from the University of Strathclyde and experience in experimental optics, quantitative data analysis, and the coordination of technical projects.

My research focused on colour centres in diamond as potential laser materials. Since then, I have worked across consulting, science communication projects, and project management for AI development within ESA mission operations and satellite simulator projects.

I enjoy understanding complex problems, working carefully with data, and making scientific and technical information clear and useful.

## Areas of experience

- **Experimental physics:** optical measurements, calibration, spectroscopy, and uncertainty analysis.
- **Data analysis and visualisation:** Python, pandas, spectral correction, and the presentation of measurement and project data.
- **Technical project coordination:** requirements analysis, agile planning, technical reporting, and collaboration across disciplines.
- **International collaboration:** research and project experience in international teams; native German and fluent English.

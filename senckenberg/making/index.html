Making of

From tracks to questions

VEBITA uses data from animal-borne sensors to study how wild animals adjust their behaviour as their environments change. While reading up on concepts related to this work, I came up with the idea to make a small game.

I borrowed the idea of rewarding unexpected answers from Krillion. The rounds start with how we track animals and move through the kinds of questions those tracks can help us ask.

Where the points come from

Together with ChatGPT, we put together a list of accepted answers for each question. Each answer points to an English Wikipedia article. The game ranks the articles in each round using three complete months of Wikimedia pageviews. The least-viewed answer earns 100 points and the most-viewed earns 20.

Pageviews measure attention to an article. The game shows the dates and view counts so you can see what each score is based on.

Putting it together

I developed the idea and content for the site. ChatGPT helped with the content, refine the wording, build the pages and connect the scoring to Wikimedia’s data. The photos are from my own collection.
